# Performance Tracking lot ledger

- **Performance Tracking is a LOT LEDGER with a summary block (Alan, 2026-10-05; Journal rows 95-96; `landry/perf_tab.py`).** One row per purchase lot, held or sold. Basis = System: the System's own purchases (ADBE, VRTX twice, PLD, V, GE, NFLX) with real entry date / price / score / band / SPY; only they feed the Rule-46 cohort (`read_performance_tab` skips Baseline rows, and any dated row with neither score nor band). Basis = Baseline: everything held at the 8/5/26 first snapshot (legacy stocks, dry-powder ETFs, cash funds, the pre-System shares of ADBE / VRTX / PLD, the five names sold 8/7/26) is measured as if held since the 8/5 close against SPY's 8/5 close -- the common baseline Alan asked for, NOT his cost basis (the 10/5 average-cost Entry Price was replaced; the return on cost lives on Current Positions); never invent an entry score for them (the 8/26 agreement). Type Stock / ETF / Cash: ETF + Cash are "Cash / Cash Equivalents" **as Current Positions' own aggregate row counts them** (Ticker cell light green C6EFCE / dark-green text 006100 while held -- my reading of Alan's "cash/cash equiv", not asked; if he meant only the four funds, clear the ETF fills and change `perf_tab.CASH_LIKE`), and every Subtotal leaves them out. A held stock's or ETF's Current Price is an INDEX/MATCH into Market Data, cash funds are $1.00, sold lots keep their sale price, and SPY now + the as-of date are the only cells `landry weekly` writes (defined names PT_SPY_Now / PT_AsOf, found by name because the block moves). The summary block under the Table is GENERATED (`perf_tab.build_block`, formulas bounded to the Table): Subtotal (held stocks) and TOTAL (everything held; its Value ties to Current Positions' combined total), then YTD and Cumulative-since-8/5 Subtotal / TOTAL (held + sold, realized + unrealized, against SPY over the same span), then Avg Annual = Cumulative TOTAL / years since 8/5 (simple, extrapolated from weeks -- not a forecast). A second section, **By source (since 8/5; held + sold)**, was added the same day (Alan, 10/5, Journal: `PERFORMANCE-TRACKING-SOURCES`): System lots only (from entry -- the System's own record, against SPY over each lot's own period), Legacy stocks, Dry-powder ETFs and Cash & money funds, plus an All sources line built as their sum. Every ledger row is in exactly one line (`perf_tab.source_of`: Basis System, else Baseline split by Type), so the four add up to the Cumulative TOTAL; the All-sources note flips to "DOES NOT add up" and the audit's `performance_tracking_ties:sources` fails when a lot has no Basis or Type. SPY on these lines is each lot's own period, so it can differ a little from the Cumulative TOTAL line's single 8/5 -> now. **Why it exists:** Alan floated selling the pre-8/5 shares of ADBE / VRTX / PLD "to make tracking under the System clean and complete from 8/5" and, once shown that the ledger already measured every lot from 8/5 and that a sale would be a discretionary override (Part 3 Hold / Core Hold-Add, Hold Through, Rule 44), decided to keep them -- "stick with the System" -- and see the System-only record this way instead. `perf_tab.BLOCK_ROWS` is 24 now; the benchmark cells and their PT_* names sit lower in the block, which is why they are found by name. My reading of Subtotal vs TOTAL (stocks vs everything including cash) was a judgment call; the other reading is active vs active + sold. All of it is the PRICE return of today's holdings: no dividends, interest, deposits or withdrawals (the Drawdown Log is the portfolio-value record). **Maintaining it:** a purchase is one System lot (`perf_tab.add_lot`: it uses a spare row, or grows the Table and rewrites the block -- NEVER `insert_rows`), a sale is `close_lot`, a PARTIAL sale of a baseline lot is `perf_tab.split_baseline_sale` (the shares sold become an Exited baseline lot, the held lot's typed Lot Shares drops by the same amount -- SPMO, 30 of 176.94 shares, 10/6/26), and **any Current Positions row taken to zero shares needs its lot closed in the same pass** (the cash funds too: FZFXX was emptied at the 10/1 settlement of the GE / NFLX buys and its lot was closed 10/7 with the 1,174.01 it held at $1.00 -- `tests/test_landry_phase5.py` fails on a green "Held" lot for a ticker that is no longer a holding); a held baseline lot's Shares follow Current Positions (quantity less the ticker's System lots) and its typed Lot Shares is the 8/5 snapshot the audit compares with, so a purchase recorded only on Current Positions is flagged (`performance_tracking_ties:baseline`); `rebase_baseline` after dividend reinvestment. `landry audit` also checks that every holding has a lot (`performance_tracking_coverage`) and the other ties (shares, value against `total_portfolio_value`, the summary's TOTAL line, the by-source split, the benchmark cells, no circular reference).
