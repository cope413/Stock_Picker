**Snapshot.** Write a JSON file in the scratchpad (format at the top of `landry/accounts.py`): `as_of`, `source` ("site read"), per account `total`, `cash` per fund, `unswept` (Fidelity cash credited but not yet in a fund), optionally `positions` {ticker: shares}, `open_orders` (see below), and `transactions` -- every row dated on or after the account's `booked_through` in `landry_accounts.json`, each with a `kind`: `external` (money in or out of the account: Chase BNK, Fidelity Electronic Funds Transfer / debit), `income` (dividend, interest, foreign tax, fee), `trade` (buy / sell / reinvestment of a position), `internal` (sweep deposit / withdrawal, core redemption / purchase, money-fund reinvest, "JOURNALED JNL VS A/C TYPES"), `other`. If Alan posted exports instead, pass them with `--chase-csv` / `--fidelity-csv` and leave `transactions` out.

**Commands.**
- `python -m landry accounts check --snapshot F [--chase-csv F] [--fidelity-csv F]` -- read-only report. Run it first.
- `python -m landry accounts apply ...` -- books each account whose cash walk closes to the cent (booked cash + new transactions = what the account shows), writes the estimate-labelled note, records posted external flows, retires a pending transfer once the same amount posts within 10 days, saves and recalcs. Exit 2 = a NEEDS ATTENTION list; an account whose walk does not close is left untouched. Then `python -m landry audit`.
- `python -m landry accounts pending --account fidelity|chase --amount N --date D --note TEXT` (`--remove` to withdraw it) -- a transfer Alan has announced that the broker does not show yet (Fidelity often lags 2-3 days). It is carried in the cash row and in the flow ledger until it posts.
- `python -m landry accounts flows` -- the external-flow ledger; its net withdrawals are what the Drawdown Log value adds back (Part 7). `apply` prints the Drawdown value (portfolio total + net withdrawals); log it with `landry drawdown add` only when Alan asks or a routine step calls for it, dated to the close the prices are from.

**Open and pending orders (Alan, 10/10: "account for/include (list) any open orders/orders pending in the weekly/month-end update summaries").** At every read, look at the Orders / Pending block on Fidelity's Activity & Orders page and at Chase's order status for 3693 (Investments > Trade / Order status), and put each open order in the snapshot's `open_orders` list for that account as one line: date placed, action, quantity, ticker, order type and limit, time in force. Use `[]` when the block was read and is empty; leave the key out only when it could not be read (the report then says "not read"). An open order has not filled, so nothing is booked for it, but it is listed in every `landry accounts` report and kept with the last read: `python -m landry accounts orders` prints them. Every weekly report and every month-end / statement-reconciliation summary given to Alan includes that list (or "none" / "not read"), and says when a listed order would change a row once filled (a sale to zero closes a lot; a money-fund sale moves cash between funds).

**What it does not do (it lists them under NEEDS ATTENTION).** A buy, sale or reinvestment: the position row (`cp_tab.insert_position` or the quantity / cost basis) and the Performance Tracking lot (`perf_tab.add_lot` / `close_lot` / `split_baseline_sale` / `rebase_baseline`) are a separate, attended pass. A money fund crossing zero: its Performance Tracking lot is opened or closed in the same pass (FZFXX's lot has been closed since 10/1; pending and unswept cash is parked on the fund in use, not on a zero row). A quantity that differs from the broker's.

**State.** `landry_accounts.json` (tracked): per account `booked_through`, the keys of transactions already counted on that date, the pending amount inside the booked cash, and the flow ledger (basis: cumulative since the 9/4/26 Drawdown Log peak). Edit it only through the commands.
